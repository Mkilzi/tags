Write it to your tag

On Android with Chrome you can use the Write to tag now button above and skip these steps.

  1. Download the free app NFC Tools (App Store or Google Play).
  2. Open it and tap Write, then Add a record, then URL / URI.
  3. Paste your link and tap OK.
  4. Tap Write and hold the top of your phone to the tag until it says done.
  5. Tap the tag with your phone to test it.

Protect your tag

So nobody else can change it: in NFC Tools go to Other, then Set password, pick a password, and tap the tag. Keep the password somewhere safe. You'll need it to update the tag later.

To change info later, come back to this page, make a new link, remove the password, and write again.
