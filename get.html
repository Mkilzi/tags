How it works
  1. Open the app and tap Add a child or Add a pet.
  2. Add their name, your contacts, and any medical notes.
  3. Pick a 4-digit PIN so only you can change it.
  4. Hold your phone to the tag. Done. Add a profile for each kid or pet.
Your info stays on your tag and your phone. We never see it.
