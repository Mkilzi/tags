Privacy Policy

Milzi Tags app and tag pages · Mind of Milzi (MILZI LLC) · Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

We don't collect or see any information you enter. Your child's or pet's info is written to the NFC tag you own and, if you use the app, saved in an encrypted profile on your own phone. It never goes to us.

What you enter

When you set up a tag, you type a name, contact names and phone numbers, and optional notes. The app or setup page turns that into a link and writes it directly to your tag. Nothing is sent to our servers. The website setup page saves nothing after you close it.

Profiles in the app

The app saves each child or pet profile, including its tag PIN, on your phone so you can update tags later. Profiles are encrypted using your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), are tied to that phone, and are not backed up to us or synced anywhere. Deleting a profile in the app removes it from your phone. We have no copy and can't recover them.

Who can see the info on a tag

Anyone who taps your tag with a phone can see the information you put on it. That's how the tag helps if your child needs help. Only add what you're comfortable with a stranger seeing. We recommend a first name only and no address.

Tag pages

When someone taps a tag, their phone opens a page on our website. The tag info is in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers do not send to the website. Our site does not use cookies, analytics, or ads. Our host may keep standard server logs (like IP address and time of visit) that do not include the tag info.

Location

On the tag page, the person who found your child can choose to text you their location. Their phone asks for permission first. The location goes straight from their phone into a text message to you. We never receive it.

NFC and PINs

The app uses your phone's NFC only when you tap a button to read or write a tag. If you lock a tag with a PIN, the PIN is stored on the tag in protected memory and in that profile on your phone.

Children

The app is meant to be used by parents and guardians. We don't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
